🌿 VastraPass

Retex Panipat | From Panipat's Waste to the World's Trust

Track textile waste. Build trust.

VastraPass is a student prototype for recording textile batches, tracking waste and sharing material information through QR-linked digital records.

🌍 The Problem

Textile waste and production records may be scattered across paper registers, bills and spreadsheets. This makes it harder to organise batch information and understand waste flows.

💡 Our Solution

Record batch details in one place, track waste quantities, calculate recycled-fibre share and create a QR-linked passport for buyers.

✨ Prototype Features

• Textile batch records
• Waste generated, reused, recycled and disposed
• Recycled-fibre calculation
• Optional document OCR assistance
• QR-linked buyer view
• Dashboard and CSV export

Prototype note: records are saved in this browser only. This demo is not a certification system and does not independently verify factory claims.

Textile waste tracking

Enter waste quantities for this batch or reporting period. All quantities are in kilograms.

Validation: reused + recycled + disposed waste must not exceed waste generated.

Optional: Read a document with OCR

Choose a clear photo of a printed or handwritten record. OCR attempts to read the text. You must check it and enter/correct the values in the form yourself. OCR may make mistakes.

OCR requires an internet connection to load the recognition library. No automatic values are saved from the image.
